# About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Robotics at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, working with [Prof. Steven Ceron](https://robotics.umich.edu/people/faculty/steven-ceron/) on control and learning for swarm robotics and multi-robot systems. Before that, I received my B.Eng. (Hons) in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Nottingham in 2024, where I began my robotics research with [Prof. Erwin (Xin) Dong](https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/engineering/departments/m3/people/xin.dong).

My research interests center on control and learning for intelligent robotic systems, from dexterous manipulation by individual robots to coordinated behavior in large-scale robot collectives. I am particularly interested in reinforcement learning, robot foundation models, and optimization-based control, with the goal of enabling robots to acquire generalizable skills, act reliably in complex environments, and collaborate effectively.

Outside of research, I’m an avid climber and outdoor enthusiast. My personal bests are V10 in bouldering and 5.13a in lead climbing. I also enjoy hiking, long-distance running, and snowboarding.
